## Advisor co-sign

Asked: is the 90-second default rest right for the Phase 1 sessions, is the cue wording ("3, 2, 1, go") right, and is the risk acceptable that wake alarms stay unproven on a device until payment while the CEO trains from paper?

Advisor's answers, word for word (2026-11-16 22:08 UTC):

> 1. 90 s rest default: CHANGE. 90 s is fine as the spike's test value, but the app must read rest from the programme data, not use one default. phase-1.json sets rest per block: 75 s for the paired main lifts, 60 s for single-leg work, 45 s for arm and shoulder accessories, 30-45 s for core. Paired lifts alternate, so each muscle still gets about 2.5 minutes. Fall back to 90 s only if the data has no value.
>
> 2. "3, 2, 1, go" in a British voice, ducking music: ACCEPT, with one addition. Add a heads-up at 10 s left that names the next exercise and weight, e.g. "10 seconds. Next, one-arm row, 12 kilos". It is the voice version of the "Dial to" card, so the dial is set before "go". Duck, never pause, the music.
>
> 3. Training from paper with own alarms until Gate 2: ACCEPT. The alarm risk is an engineering risk, not a coaching one, and a phone Clock alarm does the job. One coaching condition: paper Week 1 is the only paper week that exists. Weeks 2-6 need a printable sheet generated from phase-1.json (sessions, sets, reps, start weights, run-walk intervals), so his training matches what the app will take over and the progression isn't guessed. I'm raising that separately; it is not a Gate 0a blocker.
